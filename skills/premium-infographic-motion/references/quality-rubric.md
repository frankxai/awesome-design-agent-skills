# Premium infographic quality gate

Score the inspected export out of 30. Each category is 0–5.

## 1. First read and hierarchy

- 5: focal point and takeaway land within two seconds at feed scale.
- 3: readable after deliberate inspection; too many equal-weight elements.
- 1: title, imagery, and callouts compete.

## 2. Information architecture and accuracy

- 5: claims are correct, concise, sourced, and mapped to a meaningful visual structure.
- 3: mostly accurate but explanatory relationships are generic.
- 1: generated filler, invented labels, or misleading brand summaries.

## 3. Art direction and originality

- 5: specific, tactile, ownable, and hard to confuse with a template or stock AI output.
- 3: polished but familiar.
- 1: generic dashboard, neon AI orb, card grid, or decorative 3D filler.

## 4. Typography and micro-layout

- 5: exact copy, intentional type pairing, optical alignment, disciplined rules/connectors, and no collisions.
- 3: competent with secondary-size or spacing weaknesses.
- 1: generated text, inconsistent alignment, weak contrast, or unedited defaults.

## 5. Feed-scale accessibility

- 5: essential content survives the real crop and phone/feed preview; contrast and reading order work without audio or motion.
- 3: hook survives, but secondary content is marginal.
- 1: relies on zoom, audio, hover, or animation to become intelligible.

## 6. Editability, provenance, and motion path

- 5: editable exact master, logged sources/rights, repeatable variables, reduced-motion fallback, and purposeful motion plan.
- 3: editable but poorly systemized or incompletely logged.
- 1: flattened mystery asset with no reliable repair path.

## Decision

- 26–30: eligible for founder or independent review.
- 22–25: iterate.
- 0–21: restart the direction.

## Hard gates

Regardless of score, do not ship when any is true:

- public facts, numbers, labels, or citations are unverified;
- brand marks are generated approximations or have unclear rights;
- the actual export was not inspected;
- the 4:5/9:16 crop breaks the first read;
- essential content fails at feed/phone scale;
- motion has no purpose, reduced-motion route, or final hold;
- a paid/untested lane is described as executed;
- costs or hidden token counts are fabricated.

An author’s first pass cannot self-certify as final. Record the score as provisional until another reviewer or the user approves it.
